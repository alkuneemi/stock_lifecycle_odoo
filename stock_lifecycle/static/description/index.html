Why this module?

Internal transfers are often the root cause of inventory discrepancies when responsibilities, approvals, and shipment handovers are not clearly tracked. This module introduces a complete lifecycle workflow with accountable signatories, timestamps, and a clean visual timeline.

Approval Workflow Accountability Audit Trail Transfer Log

Workflow Steps

A clear, structured flow to ensure every stage has a responsible person and a recorded timestamp.

1) Submit Transfer Request The requester creates an internal transfer and submits it for approval.
2) Direct Manager Approval The designated manager approves or rejects the transfer request.
3) Warehouse Manager Approval Warehouse approval ensures stock and destination readiness.
4) Shipping Confirmation Shipping confirms dispatch, handover, and readiness for receiving.
5) Receiving / Final Validation The receiver confirms arrival; the process closes with final validation.

Key Features

  • Configurable signatories per picking type (Manager / Warehouse Manager / Shipping Manager).
  • Avatar-based timeline that shows the responsible person at each stage.
  • Full audit trail with timestamps for every approval/confirmation action.
  • Side transfer log with instant search by reference, number, or keywords.
  • Management-ready UI designed for operational control and internal auditing.
Accountability by design: every stage is linked to the correct signatory, not the record creator. This reduces manipulation and ensures clear responsibility.

Note: The timeline images are loaded from each signatory's partner avatar (if available), otherwise initials are shown.

Transfer Log Sidebar

A dedicated sidebar lists recent internal transfers with status badges and a live search box. Quickly jump between transfers for verification, follow-up, and auditing.

Typical Use Cases

  • Track internal transfers between main sites and remote warehouses.
  • Reduce discrepancies by enforcing approvals and logged handovers.
  • Improve audit readiness with timestamped event history.
  • Support operational control in environments with higher risk or security concerns.
